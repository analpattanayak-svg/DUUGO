About DUUGO

Healthcare, Just a Tap Away.

Our mission

DUUGO shows how simple finding a doctor and requesting an ambulance can be. This project is a frontend-only demonstration built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap 5.

It has no backend, database, payments or real booking services. Every doctor, driver and testimonial is sample content.

What DUUGO does

Doctor appointment booking

Search doctors by state, district, area and specialization, compare fees and pick a time slot.

Ambulance booking

Request an on-demand ambulance with the equipment you need — oxygen, saline, a technician or medicines.

Nursing home bed booking

Find an available bed or room, share intake details and reserve a nursing home stay.

Who DUUGO is for

Users

People who need a medical service — a patient or a family member booking on their behalf. Sign up with email, phone, name, a government ID and a password.

Service providers

Clinics, ambulance operators and nursing homes. Each provider type submits its own verification documents — registration, licence, location and capacity — before accepting bookings.

About this demo

This site is a UI demonstration only. Bookings, confirmation codes and ambulance statuses are simulated in your browser and are never transmitted anywhere.
