About doctor profiles on DUUGO

Every doctor profile on DUUGO shows the information you need to choose the right specialist with confidence. Each profile lists the doctor's specialization, years of experience, languages spoken, clinic or hospital affiliation, city and consultation fee — so you can compare doctors side by side before you book.

DUUGO covers a wide range of specializations, including general medicine, pediatrics, cardiology, dermatology, orthopedics, gynecology, neurology, psychiatry, ENT and ophthalmology. Profiles are available for doctors across states, districts and neighbourhoods in India, with a focus on Kolkata and West Bengal.

This page is a demonstration of the DUUGO interface. Doctor names, fees and testimonials are fictional sample content and no real appointment is booked through this site.
